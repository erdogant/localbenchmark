# Northwind Analytics: company overview

Northwind Analytics is a fictional company used for testing. It builds Pulse, a dashboard product for small and mid-sized businesses. The company was founded in 2014 in Utrecht, the Netherlands, by Lena Vos and Marco de Jong. Its headquarters are still in Utrecht.

The company is remote-first. Besides Utrecht, it has offices in Lisbon, which opened in 2021, and in Austin, which opened in 2023. At the end of 2025 Northwind had about 320 employees.

Northwind's mission is to make business numbers easy to read for people who are not analysts. Its main customers are retailers, clinics and logistics firms with between five and five hundred users.
