# Expense policy

Meal allowance: employees receive a daily meal allowance of 45 EUR for domestic business trips and 60 EUR for trips abroad.

Receipts: an original receipt is required for every expense above 25 EUR. Expenses below that amount can be claimed without a receipt, but the reason must be noted in the report.

Expense reports must be submitted within 14 days after the end of a trip. Reports submitted later need approval from the CFO.

Flights: economy class is standard for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class for longer flights needs written approval from the CFO.

Hotels: the nightly rate is capped at 180 EUR in major cities. Higher rates need prior approval from the line manager.
