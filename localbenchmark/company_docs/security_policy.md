# Information security policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not changed on a fixed schedule. A password is changed only when it is known or suspected to be compromised.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every staff account, including contractors who have a company login.

Laptops must use full-disk encryption. Personal devices may not store customer data.

Security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery. The on-call security engineer then leads the response.

When working away from the office, employees must connect through the company VPN before accessing internal systems.
