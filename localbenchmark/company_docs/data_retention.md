# Data retention policy

Customer support tickets are kept for 3 years after they are closed.

Invoices and billing records are kept for 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.

Employee records are kept for 5 years after the employee leaves the company.

Backups are retained for 35 days. After that they are deleted automatically.

When a customer asks for their data to be deleted, the deletion is completed within 30 days. Backup copies of the data are removed when the backup expires.
