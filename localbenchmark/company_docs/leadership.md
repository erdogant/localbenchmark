# Leadership team

The leadership team of Northwind Analytics consists of four people.

Lena Vos is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO). She co-founded the company in 2014.

Ahmed Rahimi is the Chief Technology Officer (CTO). He leads product development and the platform team. He joined in 2017.

Sophie Lindgren is the Chief Financial Officer (CFO). She joined in 2022 and is responsible for finance, legal and procurement.

Jan Pieters is the Head of People. He leads recruitment, HR and internal communication.

Each member of the leadership team reports to the CEO. The leadership team meets every two weeks.
