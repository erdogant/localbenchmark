# Customer support service levels

Incidents are classified as P1 (service down), P2 (major feature broken) or P3 (minor issue).

P1 incidents: first response within 1 hour. The resolution target is 8 hours.

P2 incidents: first response within 4 hours. The resolution target is 2 working days.

P3 incidents: first response by the next business day.

Support hours are 08:00 to 20:00 CET, Monday to Friday. Customers on the Enterprise plan receive 24/7 coverage for P1 incidents. Other customers receive P1 coverage during support hours only.
