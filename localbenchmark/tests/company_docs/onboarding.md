# Onboarding

Equipment is shipped to the new employee 5 working days before the start date. A laptop and a monitor are standard.

On the first day, new employees receive access to GitHub, Slack and the HR portal.

Each new employee gets a buddy from their team for the first two weeks.

The probation period is 3 months. During probation either side can end the contract with one week's notice.

The first week includes a security training session that must be completed before production access is granted.
