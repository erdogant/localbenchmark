# Pulse pricing

Starter plan: 49 EUR per month. Includes up to 5 users and 10 dashboards.

Team plan: 199 EUR per month. Includes up to 25 users, unlimited dashboards and API access.

Enterprise plan: custom pricing, agreed in a written contract. Includes single sign-on and a named account manager.

Annual billing: customers who pay for a full year in advance receive a 10% discount on the monthly price.

Free trial: every new account gets a free trial of 14 days on the Team plan. No credit card is needed to start the trial.
