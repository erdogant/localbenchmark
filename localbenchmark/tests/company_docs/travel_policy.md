# Travel policy

All business travel is booked through the company travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed.

Trains are preferred for journeys shorter than 4 hours. For longer distances, employees should check whether flying is cheaper and faster door to door.

Trips with a total cost above 2,000 EUR need approval from the head of the employee's department before booking. The approval request is made in the travel portal and is usually answered within one working day.

Travel insurance is included for every trip booked through the portal. Employees must carry their company ID when travelling.
