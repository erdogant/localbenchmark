# Northwind history

2014: Lena Vos and Marco de Jong found Northwind Analytics in Utrecht.

2016: The first product, Pulse dashboard, launches in beta with 12 customers.

2019: Northwind closes a Series B funding round of 22 million EUR. The round is led by a Dutch growth fund.

2021: The Lisbon office opens. Most of the support team moves there.

2023: The Austin office opens to serve customers in North America.

2025: Northwind reaches about 320 employees and passes 4,000 paying customers.

The company has been profitable since 2024, but it does not publish revenue figures or profit numbers outside its annual report to shareholders.
